About Clyntec

Kenya's Kyocera Printer & IT Partner Since 2020

Clyntec is a Nairobi-based technology company. Printers are our core speciality — as an authorised Kyocera partner, we sell, install and service printer fleets for businesses across Kenya — alongside IT support and networking, CCTV and security systems, VoIP communications, and custom software. Rather than one-off jobs, we work with the same clients for years through Annual Maintenance Contracts and ongoing support.

Mission & Vision

Why Clyntec Exists

Our Mission

To equip Kenyan businesses, in every region and not only in the capital, with dependable printing, networking, security and communications infrastructure. We deliver this through authorised manufacturer partnerships, transparent pricing and structured maintenance contracts that prevent downtime rather than react to it.

Our Vision

To be a leading and trusted technology partner for Kenyan business, recognised in every major town for consistent service standards, genuine parts and accountable support, regardless of distance from Nairobi.

Integrity
